# Getting help

| You want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Ask how to do something, or share an idea | [Discussions](../../discussions) |
| Report something that's broken | [A bug report](../../issues/new/choose) |
| Report a security problem | [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Please don't use public issues |
| Read how it works | [docs/](docs/) |

Before you ask, check the [FAQ](README.md#faq) and [troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).
The log at `~/Library/Logs/IPTether/iptether.log` usually explains what happened. Remove account
IDs, subscription IDs and IP addresses before you post any of it.

IPTether is maintained by And AI Platforms and community contributors. We answer as time allows,
and there's no guaranteed response time or paid support.
